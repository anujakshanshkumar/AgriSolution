About us

Agri Solution is a digital support platform created to empower farmers with reliable agricultural information, practical guidance, and direct access to essential resources for improving farm productivity. Our mission is to bridge the gap between traditional farming knowledge and modern technology, ensuring every farmer makes informed decisions for a sustainable and prosperous agricultural future.

Mission | Vission | Objective

Mission

To empower farmer with reliable information , modern technology,and access to essential resources to improve productivity and income.

Vission

We aim for future where every farmer is successful , farming sustainably , and has easy access to reliable information. This will lead to better lives for farming families and strong secure food supplies for all

Objectives

  • Deliver transparent and reliable agriculture-related information
  • Actively encourage farmers to adopt sustainable and planet-friendly practices.
  • Balance modern farming with traditional wisdom
  • Improve market connectivity and profitability for farmers
  • Ensure easy access to government schemes and assistance

About the Department

The Department of Agriculture, Government of Chhattisgarh, works with a clear vision to support farmers and strengthen the agricultural ecosystem across the state. The department provides scientific guidance, technical support, and access to reliable resources that help farmers improve productivity and adopt sustainable practices. Along with promoting new innovations, it also respects and preserves traditional farming knowledge that has shaped rural livelihoods for generations. By connecting farmers with modern tools, government schemes, accurate market information, and expert advisory services, the department aims to build a stable, progressive, and self-reliant agricultural community..

  • •  Integrating modern scientific methods while preserving valuable traditional farming wisdom
  • •  Providing access to government schemes and financial support
  • •  Supporting irrigation development and water-efficient technologies
  • •  Delivering crop advisory and weather-based recommendations
  • •  Implementing soil testing and nutrient management programs
  • •  Monitoring market trends and offering fair price information
  • •  Conducting farmer training, workshops, and field demonstrations

Our Purpose

Building a more prosperous Agriculture Future

"The Agri-Solution Portal stands as a dedicated, independent information resource committed to empowering the agricultural community across Chhattisgarh. Our platform serves as a vital bridge, simplifying the vast and complex landscape of farming knowledge. We meticulously gather and organize diverse information, including comprehensive state and central government scheme guidelines, accurate daily market price forecasts, localized seasonal crop advisories, and expert-vetted best practices for soil and pest management. Our mission is to facilitate informed decision-making by placing reliable, up-to-date data directly into the hands of farmers, fostering sustainability, increasing productivity, and ultimately driving long-term economic prosperity for every user."

Key Focus Areas

Sustainable farming and better crop productivity

  • Guidance on climate-smart and environment-friendly farming
  • Support to improve soil fertility and reduce input costs
  • Encouraging efficient water use and modern irrigation methods
  • Sharing research-based practices to increase yield and profit

Strong market Access and Farmer Income Growth

  • Daily market price updates for major crops
  • Direct connection with buyers, dealers, and mandis
  • Fair-trade marketplace support and business guidance
  • Training for financial planning and income diversification

Technology Support & Easy Access to Govt. Resources

  • One place for schemes, subsidies and online registrations
  • Weather alerts, crop care tips and pest advisory
  • Access to quality seeds, fertilizers & modern machinery
  • Expert help through helplines and field-level support

Core Values

Farmers Come First

Every decision we make begins with the needs of farmers. We aim to offer solutions that are practical, affordable, and useful in real situations—not just theory.

Respect for Both Traditional & Modern Farming

We believe that traditional knowledge is the foundation of agriculture. We support modern techniques only when they genuinely help farmers and protect the wisdom that has sustained farming for generations..

Grow Without Damaging the Future

We encourage environmentally responsible farming that protects soil, water, and biodiversity for the next generation..

Reliable and Practical Information

We focus on sharing clear and useful knowledge that supports real farming need. Every piece of information is simple to understand and directly helpful for field-level decisions, so farmers can act with confidence and without confusion.
