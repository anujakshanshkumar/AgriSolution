A starting point for better care

A photo can suggest possible causes, but cannot confirm a disease. Lighting, crop variety, soil and weather can all affect the assessment.

  • Include the whole leaf and visible symptoms.
  • Use a sharp, well-lit photo without filters.
  • Consult a local agricultural officer if symptoms spread or the crop is at risk.

For any chemical treatment, use locally approved products and follow the label and qualified agricultural advice.
