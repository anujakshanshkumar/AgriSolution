Soil profile of Chhattisgarh

Regional soil types, their properties, and what to consider for your field.

Regional guide — soil can vary within a farm

Local soil conditions

Regional model estimate for Raipur; not a field soil test.

Topsoil moisture--Model estimate, 0–1 cm layer
Soil temperature--Model estimate near the surface
Sandy loam

Matasi

Reddish-yellow and lighter in texture. Check drainage and organic matter; crops such as pulses and millets may suit local conditions.

Clay-rich

Kanhar

Fine-textured soil that can hold moisture. Avoid working it when very wet, and use a soil test to guide crop and nutrient decisions.

Clay loam

Dorsa

An intermediate soil type with a mix of lighter and heavier characteristics. Paddy and other crops may suit depending on water and field conditions.

Upland soil

Bhata

Coarse, often reddish soil on uplands. Consider moisture conservation and locally suitable crops; test soil before adding nutrients.

Riverine soil

Kachhar

Alluvial soil associated with river areas. Soil depth, drainage, and nutrient levels differ by site, so check your plot before planning.

For crop selection and fertilizer application, use a current soil-health test and advice from your local agriculture office or Krishi Vigyan Kendra.
