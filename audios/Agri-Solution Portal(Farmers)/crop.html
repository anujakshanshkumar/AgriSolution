Kharif Crops

Chhattisgarh's Kharif season, during the monsoon (June-Oct(Sown June-July , Harvested sep-oct)), focuses on water-loving crops like Paddy (Rice), making it the "Rice Bowl" of India , alongside important crops such as Soybean, Maize, Urad (Black Gram), Arhar (Pigeon Pea), and various millets (like Kodo-Kutki), crucial for food and farmer income, with paddy dominating the landscape

Rabi Crops

In Chhattisgarh, Rabi crops are winter-sown crops like wheat, gram (chickpea), and linseed, planted from October-November and harvested in spring (March-April). These crops need less water and thrive in cool, dry weather, with chickpea and lathyrus being very common, along with other pulses, millets, and oilseeds, filling the fields after the monsoon rice harvest.

Paddy (Rice)

Cereals/Grains Chhattisgarh is India's Rice Bowl" (Dhaan Ka Katora)" because it grows huge amounts of paddy (unmilled rice), making it a top rice producer with ideal conditions for the crop, which is central to the state's farming, culture, and food, with unique varieties

Maize

Cereals/Grains Maize (corn) is a vital crop in Chhattisgarh, second only to rice, grown widely for food, feed, and industry, especially in tribal areas like Bastar, providing income and nutrition through flour, daliya, and starch products

Kodo Millet

Cereals/Grains Kodo and Kutki are nutritious, drought-resistant millets (small grains) traditionally grown in Chhattisgarh, especially by tribal communities, that are easy to grow in tough conditions and great for health

Bajra (Pearl Millet)

Cereals/Grains Bajra is a hardy and low-cost crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s dry and light soils. It needs very little water and can survive hot and dry conditions. The crop matures in about 3–4 months. Bajra grains are used for roti and fodder, making it useful for both food and livestock.

Jowar (Sorghum)

Cereals/Grains Jowar is a hardy crop that grows well even with less water. In Chhattisgarh, farmers grow it on light and medium soils during the kharif season. It is easy to maintain, needs fewer inputs, and is ready to harvest in about 3–4 months. Jowar grains are used for roti, fodder, and feed, making it a useful crop for both income and livestock.

Soyabean

Oilseeds Soybean is a popular kharif crop in Chhattisgarh that grows well with moderate rainfall. It needs well-drained soil and very little irrigation. The crop is ready in about 3–3.5 months. Soybean gives good income because its seeds are rich in oil and protein. Farmers also use its leftover plant parts as good-quality fodder for animals.

Groundnut

Oilseeds Groundnut is a useful kharif crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s sandy and light soils. It needs moderate water and good sunlight. The crop is ready to harvest in about 3–4 months. Groundnut gives good income because it has high oil content, and its plants also provide nutritious fodder for animals. It is a profitable crop for small and medium farmers.

Sesamum (Til)

Oilseeds Sesame is an easy and profitable crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s light, sandy soils. It needs very little water and can tolerate dry conditions. The crop is ready in about 2.5–3 months. Sesame seeds have high oil content and always get good market prices. Farmers like it because it is a “kam kharch, achha munafa” crop that gives steady income with less effort

Sunflower

Oilseeds Sunflower is a bright and profitable crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s light to medium soils. It needs very little water and grows fast, ready in just 2.5–3 months. Sunflower seeds give high-quality oil with good market demand. Because of its strong roots and low input cost, farmers call it a “kam paani, zyada munafa” crop. Surajmukhi is a smart choice for farmers who want quick harvest and better income.

Arhar (Pigeon Pea)

Pulses Arhar is a strong and reliable crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s medium and light soils. It needs very little irrigation and can handle dry conditions easily. The crop takes about 5–6 months to mature. Arhar gives high-quality dal that always has good market demand. Farmers like it because it is a “kam paani, pakki aamdani” crop that improves soil health and gives steady profit.

Urad (Black Gram)

Pulses Urad is a quick and profitable crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s light to medium soils. It needs very little water and can easily tolerate dry weather. The crop is ready in just 2–3 months. Urad gives high-quality dal with strong market demand.

Moong (Green Gram)

Pulses Moong is a fast-growing and profitable crop that fits well in Chhattisgarh’s light and medium soils. It needs very little water and grows easily even in dry conditions. The crop is ready in just 2–2.5 months. Moong dal always gets good market price

Cotton

Other Crop Cotton is a valuable cash crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s medium and black soils. It needs good sunlight and moderate water. The crop takes about 5–6 months to mature. Cotton gives high market returns because of its strong demand in the textile industry

Sugarcane

Other Crop Sugarcane is a high-yield, high-income crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s fertile, moist soils. It needs regular water and good sunlight. The crop takes about 10–12 months to mature but gives a very heavy harvest. Sugarcane has strong demand in sugar mills and jaggery units.

Kulthi

Pulses Kulthi is a strong, low-cost crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s dry, rocky, and light soils. It needs very little water and survives even in tough conditions. The crop is ready in about 3–3.5 months. Kulthi dal has good demand for its health benefits.

Wheat

Careals/Grains Wheat is the main rabi crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s fertile and well-drained soils. It needs cool weather and timely irrigation. The crop is ready in about 4–5 months. Wheat grains always have stable market demand.

Barley

Careals/Grains Barley is an easy and hardy rabi crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s light and medium soils. It needs cool weather and moderate water. The crop is ready in about 3–4 months. Barley grains are used for food, fodder, and brewing, so they always have demand.

Gram (Chana)

Pulses Gram (Chana) Gram is a strong and profitable rabi crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s medium and light soils. It needs cool weather and very little water. The crop is ready in about 4–5 months. Gram dal has good market demand and is rich in protein.

Masoor (Lentil)

Pulses Masoor is a quick-growing rabi crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s medium and light soils. It needs cool weather and very little water. The crop is ready in about 3–4 months. Masoor dal always has good market demand. Farmers

Peas

Careals/Grains (Matar / Green Peas) Peas are a nutritious and profitable rabi crop that grow well in Chhattisgarh’s fertile, well-drained soils. They need cool weather and moderate water. The crop is ready in about 3–4 months. Peas have good market demand and are used for fresh vegetables and dals

Mustard

Oilseeds Mustard is an important and profitable rabi oilseed crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s fertile and well-drained soils. It thrives in cool weather and needs moderate water. The crop matures in about 3–4 months and gives high-quality seeds with excellent oil content. Mustard has good market demand, making it a reliable source of income for farmers.

Linseed

Oilseeds Mustard is an important and profitable rabi oilseed crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s fertile and well-drained soils. It thrives in cool weather and needs moderate water. The crop matures in about 3–4 months and gives high-quality seeds with excellent oil content. Mustard has good market demand, making it a reliable source of income for farmers.

Sunflower

Oilseeds Sunflower can also be grown as a rabi crop in Chhattisgarh on well-drained soils with irrigation. It needs cool weather, plenty of sunlight, and moderate water. The crop matures in about 3–3.5 months and gives high-quality seeds rich in oil.

Oata

Careals/Grains Oats is a nutritious and profitable rabi crop that grows well in Chhattisgarh’s cool, well-drained soils. It needs moderate water and can tolerate light frost. The crop matures in about 3–3.5 months. Oats grains are rich in fiber and have good demand for food and fodder.

Vegetable

Other Crops Rabi season is ideal for growing cool-weather vegetables in Chhattisgarh. Popular crops include: Cauliflower & Cabbage – Need fertile soil, give high market value. Carrot & Radish – Grow well in light soil, ready in 2–3 months. Peas & Beans – Provide fresh dal and vegetables, easy to grow. Spinach, Fenugreek & Leafy Greens – Quick-growing, nutritious, and good for continuous income. Tomato & Brinjal – Can be grown with irrigation, high demand in local markets.
